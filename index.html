Happy Teachers' Day!

Sir. Jeffrey Macabare
Dear Sir. Macabare,

Today is a small chance to say something we do not say enough: thank you.

Thank you for the patience you bring to every lesson, and for explaining things again and again until they finally make sense. You never made a question feel small, and because of that, we learned to ask more of them.

You do more than teach a subject. You teach us to think for ourselves, to keep trying when something is hard, and to believe that we can improve. Many of us walked into your class unsure of ourselves and walked out a little more confident.

We know teaching takes long hours of planning, checking, and caring that students rarely see. We see it now, and we are grateful for every bit of it.

As we get closer to the end of our school years, we want you to know that what you taught us will stay with us long after the last bell. Your lessons will show up in the work we do, the choices we make, and the way we treat other people.

Thank you for being the kind of teacher we will remember. Happy Teachers' Day, Sir.

With gratitude and respect,

G12 Hopper
